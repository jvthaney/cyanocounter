Customize counters for plankton taxa



  • 1. Enter your information above and click "Submit Info" to log it.
    • Enter squares counted (out of 1,000) when you finish. For a partial row: completed full rows × 50 + squares in the partial row. E.g. 5 rows + 23 squares = 273.
    • If a count threshold is set, a teal progress bar tracks your running total and turns orange when you've hit it. You can then stop or complete the slide.
  • 2. Count colonies/filaments (not cells). Click "Add counter +" for each taxon.
  • 3. When done, click "Export data" to download a .CSV file.
